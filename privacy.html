Privacy Policy

Why The World · Effective date: November 13, 2026

1. Summary

“Why The World” is a private tool that publishes the creator’s own videos to the creator’s own accounts. We do not collect, store, sell or share personal data of other people.

2. What data is processed

  • Account owner’s access tokens. To publish through official APIs (for example the TikTok Content Posting API), the tool stores the access and refresh tokens that the account owner grants for their own account. They are kept only on the owner’s own computer, are not shared with anyone, and are used only to upload and publish the owner’s videos.
  • Basic account information returned by the platform for the owner’s own account (such as account ID or username), used only to confirm which account a video is posted to.

3. What we do not do

  • We do not collect data from viewers, followers or any other users.
  • We do not read other people’s profiles, messages, contacts or comments.
  • We do not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers on this website.
  • We do not sell or transfer any data to third parties.

4. Data retention and deletion

Tokens are kept only as long as publishing is needed. The account owner can revoke access at any time in the platform’s settings (for example, TikTok → Settings and privacy → Security → Apps / Manage app permissions), after which the stored tokens stop working and are deleted.

5. Children

The tool is not directed at children and does not collect any data from them.

6. Changes

This policy may be updated. The current version is always available at this address, with the effective date shown above.

7. Contact

Privacy questions: send a direct message to @whytheworldfacts on Instagram.
